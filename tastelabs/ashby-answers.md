```text
The central bottleneck in AI today is not model capacity—it is evaluation in subjective domains.

But when frontier models enter creative, aesthetic, or subjective territory—visual design, editorial cadence, brand identity, layout balance, and voice—they collapse to the mean. Without rigorous, granular measurement, models optimize toward bland, homogenized mediocrity: what the ecosystem rightly calls "AI slop."

Taste Labs’ core thesis is profound because it recognizes that taste is not an uncrackable mystery or an elusive human spark—it is fundamentally a measurement and decomposition problem. What excites me most:
1. Cracking the Evaluation Bottleneck: Turning fuzzy qualitative criteria (e.g., visual balance, typography hierarchy, tone consistency) into deterministic, multi-tier evaluation suites is the highest-leverage engineering challenge in applied AI.
2. The Dual-Sided Ecosystem Impact: Partnering directly with frontier labs to supply RL post-training environments while simultaneously providing agent-layer companies with runtime context and taste verifiers creates an indispensable infrastructure layer for the next decade of AI.
3. The Polymath Startup Culture: Thais's vision of assembling high-agency polymaths who bridge systems engineering, creative sensitivity, and startup execution is the exact culture where I do my best work.

I live in San Francisco, work with autonomous coding and evaluation harnesses daily, and want nothing more than to be on-site every day with your team ending AI slop and giving models real taste.
```
